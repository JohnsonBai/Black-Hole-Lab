10.0 M☉

Black Hole Mass

Mass determines the strength of a black hole's gravitational field and the size of its event horizon. M☉ — Solar Mass. M☉ means the mass of the Sun. It is a convenient unit for measuring the mass of a black hole.

A more massive black hole has a larger event horizon.

Try moving the slider and watch how the black hole changes.

0.000

Black Hole Spin

A rotating black hole is described by its dimensionless spin parameter.

A value of 0 means no rotation, while larger values represent faster rotation.

Increase the spin and observe how the black hole's properties change.

5.0 rₛ

Impact Parameter

The impact parameter describes how close a light ray would pass to the black hole if gravity did not bend its path. rₛ — Schwarzschild Radius. rₛ is the characteristic radius of a black hole. For a non-spinning black hole, it is the radius of the event horizon.

A smaller impact parameter means that the light passes closer to the black hole and experiences stronger gravitational deflection.

Move the slider closer to the critical value and watch the light become strongly deflected.

5.00 rₛ

Time Dilation

According to general relativity, gravity affects the passage of time.

Clocks closer to a black hole appear to run more slowly compared with clocks farther away.

Move the slider closer to the event horizon and compare the two clocks below.
